Get Atelier

Sideload Atelier yourself

Nothing is signed on this page. Get Atelier's unsigned IPA or its source, and sign it with your own signer and certificate.

You'll need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • A certificate: a .p12 file, its password, and a .mobileprovision that lists your iPhone.
  • An app to sign it with, once: Sideloadly on a Mac or PC, or Feather, AltStore or SideStore on your iPhone.
  1. 1

    Get the IPA or the source

    The newest release is unsigned, so your signer signs it with your certificate.

  2. 2

    Sign and install it

    Open the IPA in your signer, or install Atelier from its source there.

    Use the certificate you'll sign your apps with

    Atelier signs its own updates with it, and iOS only installs an update over Atelier when it's signed for the same App ID.

    Step-by-step guide for each app
  3. 3

    Open Atelier

    If iOS asks, trust the developer in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, or turn on Developer Mode in Settings › Privacy & Security, which restarts the iPhone. Then Get Ready helps you add your certificate and your sources.
