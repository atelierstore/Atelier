Install with your Apple ID

Your iPhone signs Atelier with your Apple ID and installs it by itself, with two free apps from the App Store. No computer, and no certificate to buy.

This iPhone has iOS

The installer needs iOS 27 or later. Update in Settings › General › Software Update, or install Atelier with a signing service in the meantime.

You'll need

  • An iPhone with iOS 27 or later, on Wi‑Fi. iOS only lets the installer reach your iPhone over Wi‑Fi.
  • Your Apple ID, free or paid. With a free one, Atelier renews itself every week, and takes 1 of the 3 places your iPhone has for apps signed with free Apple IDs.
  • rootshell and LocalDevVPN, two free apps from the App Store. rootshell runs the installer, and LocalDevVPN lets it reach your iPhone's install service.
Your Apple ID stays on your iPhone

The installer runs in rootshell. Your password only goes to Apple. Atelier gets Apple's sign-in token instead, and keeps it in its Keychain to renew itself. Nothing goes through this site. The installer is open source: atelierstore/atelier-install.

  1. 1
    Do these steps on your iPhone
    1. Open the Camera on your iPhone and point it at the code.
    2. Tap the link that shows up. This page opens there, where you'll copy the line into rootshell.

    Get the two apps

    Both are free on the App Store. rootshell runs the installer. Keep LocalDevVPN afterwards: Atelier needs it to install and renew your apps.

    rootshell LocalDevVPN
  2. 2

    Turn on Developer Mode

    In Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode. Your iPhone restarts, then asks you to confirm.

  3. 3

    Install Atelier

    First, in rootshell's Settings › General, turn on Location Diary Mode with its Mode set to Session Only, so it keeps running while you're in Settings. Session Only turns itself off whenever rootshell closes, such as when your iPhone restarts.

    Then copy this line into rootshell. It downloads the installer, gives it your iPhone's Wi‑Fi address, which pairing needs, and installs Atelier's newest release, checked against its SHA‑256.

    AtelierNewest release

    Reading Atelier's newest release…

    Copy and open rootshell

    Safari asks to open LocalDevVPN first. It connects, then switches to rootshell by itself. The first time, allow it to add its VPN.

    Paste the line in rootshell and press Return. Then do what rootshell asks:

    1. Pair. In Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, tap Pair with Atelier and enter your passcode. Settings then asks for a code. Switch to rootshell to read it, then go back to Settings and type it.
    2. Go back to rootshell and press Return. LocalDevVPN is connected already.
    3. Sign in with your Apple ID, its password, and the code Apple sends if it asks. rootshell doesn't show the password as you type it.
    4. Stay in rootshell while it signs and installs Atelier.
    If it stops

    Copy the same line again. The installer picks up where it left off. If nothing happens for a minute, press Control-C in rootshell first.

    If it waits after pairing

    If nothing shows for a minute after you press Return, your iPhone's pairing service has moved to another port, which can happen after a restart. Press Control-C. A Bonjour browser such as Discovery – DNS-SD Browser, free on the App Store, shows the port under _remotepairing._tcp. Copy the line again, and add --port and that number at the end.

  4. 4

    Open Atelier

    Trust your Apple ID in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, then open Atelier. It opens signed in and already paired, so it installs your apps without iOS's alert, and renews itself every week on Wi‑Fi.
