Install Atelier with a signing service

This page signs Atelier with the certificate your signing service gave you, in Safari on your iPhone or on a computer, and iOS installs it. Atelier then opens with your certificate ready to install your apps and update itself.

You'll need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Open this page in Safari on it, or on a computer and scan a code with the iPhone at the end.
  • A certificate from a signing service for your iPhone, and its password.

Where your certificate goes

  • Your browser signs Atelier with it, on your iPhone or computer. It isn't sent anywhere to be signed.
  • With Include Certificate on, it goes inside Atelier. This site keeps the signed Atelier at a private address until iOS downloads it, then deletes it, after 10 minutes at most. On a computer, the code your iPhone scans holds that address: anyone with it can download Atelier and your certificate, so don't share it.
  • Nothing is logged, and the page keeps nothing once you leave it.
