Sign in with a free Apple ID, as with AltStore and SideStore, and Atelier renews your apps every week without reinstalling them. Custom Install… changes an app's name, icon or certificate and keeps its data, Open works for every app, and apps Atelier reinstalls keep their place on the Home Screen.

## What's new

- **Sign in with an Apple ID.** Settings › Installs › Certificates › + › Sign In with Apple ID, free or paid, next to importing a certificate. Atelier makes the certificate on your iPhone and gets each app its own profile from Apple. You can sign in with several Apple IDs, and each free one has its own 10 new apps a week.
- **Renewed every week.** A free Apple ID's apps stop opening after 7 days, so Atelier renews each one two days before, in about two seconds, without reinstalling it. It renews when you open Atelier, in background refreshes and during automatic updates, with LocalDevVPN connected. The Library's Renew section only lists what it couldn't renew by itself, and a notification warns you the day before an app stops opening.
- **Offload and Reinstall.** A free Apple ID allows 3 apps at a time. Offload takes an app off your iPhone and keeps its data in Atelier, and Reinstall puts both back. When the 3 places are taken, Make Room offloads one and carries on with the install. Any app but Atelier can be offloaded.
- **Apple ID and certificate pages.** Each shows its apps on this iPhone, which one stops opening next and when Atelier renews it, and for a free Apple ID how many new apps it allows this week. The Library's filter shows one certificate's or Apple ID's apps at a time.
- **Custom Install….** One sheet in place of Install a Copy… and Sign Again With…: sign an app again with another certificate, change its name, icon tint, picture or version and keep its data, install an app under a name and icon of your own, or add a copy.
- **Home Screen spots.** When Atelier has to delete an app to install it again, it puts it back where it was on your Home Screen. iOS doesn't report the open spaces you leave between icons, so mark the ones to keep in Settings › Home Screen › Open Spaces. This needs the setup.
- **Open any app.** Open works for every app on your iPhone, including ones signed elsewhere, from the Library, app pages and Browse.
- **Welcome and Checkup.** The first launch says what Atelier is and lists what it needs on one Get Ready page. Settings › Installs › Checkup checks your certificate, the install server, the setup and your sources at any time.
- **Private GitHub repos.** Where Atelier said HTTP 404, it now asks you to sign in to GitHub, with a button. GitHub release links download through your account.
- **Links you can tap.** Install, source, app and search links have https:// pages on Atelier's website that open Atelier, with the app's icon, name and version. App and install links can name their source, and Atelier adds it first.
- **Apps installed from a link** remember it, so Custom Install… and Re-sign download them again, and their page offers Copy Link and Share….
- **Copies of Atelier** keep their own data, can bring your certificate with them, and know your iPhone's UDID. Any Atelier now reads the UDID from a profile that lists only your iPhone.
- **Clearer messages.** Errors and questions say whether an app keeps its data and what to do next. iOS's own words stay in the install log.
- **Atelier's website** offers three ways to get Atelier: signed in Safari with your certificate, from a signing service, or sideloaded yourself. Signing services have a page on how to offer it.

## Fixes

- Installs you tap no longer wait behind ones left unfinished from before, which could install an update you were holding back. Automatic updates leave out updates that ask for a new permission.
- Reinstall keeps downloading when you leave Atelier right after tapping it.
- A copy of Atelier no longer shares Atelier's sources and Library, which wiped its Open button and certificate.

## Updating from 0.4.0-alpha

Atelier's update shows in the Library. You can also install this IPA over it, signed with the same certificate and profile. Your sources, certificates and apps stay.

## Installing

Get Atelier from [its website](https://atelierstore.app), or sign the IPA below with your own certificate and install it with a tool such as Sideloadly, Feather, AltStore or SideStore ([guide](https://github.com/atelierstore/Atelier/blob/main/docs/install.md)). Requires iOS 17 or later.

## Known limits

- Background installs and automatic updates need Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN, and renewing needs LocalDevVPN.
- Apps signed with an Apple ID only install through the setup (Settings › Installs › Setup).
- A free Apple ID allows 3 apps at a time on your iPhone and 10 new apps a week. Renewing an app takes one of the 10 again once its App ID has ended, about every 10 days.
- Apps signed with an Apple ID don't keep their App Groups yet, so a widget or extension that shares data with its app may show nothing.
- Atelier itself can't be signed with an Apple ID.
- Apple has refused some free Apple ID sign-ins on and off since October (error -80045).
- Atelier updates itself only with a profile for the App ID it was installed with.
- Atelier's releases are pre-releases for now, so keep Include Pre-releases on for its source.
